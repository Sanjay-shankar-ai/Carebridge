role: patient

Waiting for follow-up answers after an abnormal screening can feel heavy and frightening.
A screening report alone does not diagnose cancer; a specialist visit gives real clarity and lets you ask questions at your own pace.
If distress feels overwhelming or unsafe, contact your care team or local emergency services right away.
