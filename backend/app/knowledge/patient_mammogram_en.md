A screening mammogram takes X-ray pictures of breast tissue to look for areas that may need a closer look.
BI-RADS is a standard scoring scale radiologists use to describe how clearly an image looks and whether follow-up is recommended.
BI-RADS Category 4 means a finding needs further checking, often with a targeted ultrasound or a core needle biopsy, because an image alone cannot tell the full story.
Only a physician reviewing your images and any follow-up tests can explain what the findings mean for you.
