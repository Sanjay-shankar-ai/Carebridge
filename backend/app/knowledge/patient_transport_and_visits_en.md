Patients who need help reaching the clinic can request medical transport. Pickup is requested 60 minutes before the appointment start time and confirmed by a care coordinator.
If an appointment is rescheduled, the transport pickup time is updated automatically and sent back to the coordinator for confirmation.
Patients may always ask to move or cancel an appointment without pressure; the care team stays available to help find a workable time.
