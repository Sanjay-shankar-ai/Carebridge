A screening journey is only complete when care happens.

This is CareBridge — closing the screening-to-care loop.

The patient signs in and speaks naturally with the role-aware AI assistant.

It captures the screening need and creates a real task for the Care Coordinator.

Watch the coordinator notification arrive — from a persisted task, not from a chat promise.

The coordinator creates the screening order.

Notice Stage One remains pending: an order alone is not proof that screening took place.

Once the performed screening is recorded, the timeline advances — and Stage One turns green.

Next, the coordinator uploads the actual screening report as a PDF.

Upload alone does not complete Stage Two.

The report is finalized and validated. Only then does Stage Two complete.

The patient is notified, and that notification is recorded.

The patient asks what the report means.

The assistant retrieves the actual case report and explains it in clear language.

It does not diagnose.

Delivery of that explanation is recorded as evidence for Stage Four — it does not assume the patient understood everything.

For the remaining journey, we switch to a pre-seeded synthetic case.

A doctor reviews the findings and records the clinical decision.

The patient receives the next-step confirmation.

A referral is created, then accepted by the receiving provider.

Stage Nine turns green only after the doctor and patient confirm the same appointment version.

After the consultation is recorded by the doctor, Stage Ten completes.

Treatment monitoring, follow-up, and documentation advance from their own recorded evidence.

Now test the boundary: the Care Coordinator attempts to close the case — and is blocked.

Only an authorized doctor can complete the closure review and sign off.
